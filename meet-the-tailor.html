Meet David Windross

David Windross has been immersed in the tailoring business since he was 16 years old, beginning his career as the lead designer for a major tailoring firm. Even then, he possessed an unwavering passion to build his own brand. After 20 years of honing his craft and working under someone else's name, he decided it was time to step out and launch Windross Tailoring and Designs.

With decades of high-level experience, David doesn't just cut a suit or style it for the business of it—he does it for the pure passion of the work. Every garment is crafted to help the wearer look prepared, confident, and unforgettable.

Built on Craft, Detail, and Personal Service

Windross Tailoring exists for clients who do not want a generic fit or a rushed experience. Some clients arrive knowing exactly what they want. Others need help choosing the right fabric, colour, fit, and finish. Both are welcome. The Windross process is designed to guide each client from idea to fitting to final garment with care.

Our Tailoring Philosophy

Listen First

Every suit begins with understanding the client, the occasion, the timeline, and the impression they want to create.

Fit With Purpose

The goal is not just to make the suit fit the body, but to make the client feel confident wearing it.

Finish With Excellence

Details matter — fabric, colour, lapels, trouser style, proportions, and final presentation all work together.

How the Windross Experience Works

  1. Style Conversation

    Discussing your needs, vision, and the specific event.

  2. Fabric and Design Direction

    Selecting the right textures, colors, and cuts.

  3. Measurements or Fitting

    Precision measuring either in-person or guided online.

  4. Tailoring and Refinement

    Crafting the suit with care and making necessary adjustments.

  5. Final Presentation

    Delivery of a suit that fits perfectly and feels incredible.
